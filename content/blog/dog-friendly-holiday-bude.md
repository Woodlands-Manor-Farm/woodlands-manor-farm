## Dog-friendly beaches near Bude

North Cornwall's beaches are the big draw — and plenty of them welcome dogs all year round.

- **Duckpool** — our nearest beach, just two miles away. A wild, National Trust cove at the foot of the Coombe Valley, dog-friendly all year, with rock pools, a stream and space to run.
- **Sandymouth & Northcott Mouth** — two spectacular National Trust beaches a few miles north, both dog-friendly year round. Huge expanses of sand at low tide.
- **Widemouth Bay** — golden sand and gentle surf a little south of Bude, with a dog-friendly stretch.

A quick word on Bude's two **town beaches**, Summerleaze and Crooklets: they're lovely, but they carry a **seasonal dog ban** through the main summer season (roughly Easter to the end of September). Out of season, dogs are welcome — and the year-round beaches above more than make up for it in summer. Restrictions do change, so it's always worth a glance at the signage when you arrive. We've mapped our favourites, with directions from the farm gate, on our guide to [local beaches & walks](/beaches-and-walks-near-bude/).

## The best dog walks near Bude

You don't even have to get in the car. Woodlands sits in 15 acres of its own woodland, with walks straight from the door — carpeted in bluebells in April and May, and beautiful in any season. Dogs love it, and so do we.

Just two miles away, the **South West Coast Path** delivers some of the most dramatic clifftop walking in England, between Hartland Point and Bude — sea stacks, hidden coves and waterfalls the whole way. For something gentler, the **Bude Canal** towpath is flat, scenic and easy on older dogs (and older legs).

![Sea thrift above Duckpool, a dog-friendly beach near Bude all year round](/images/bude/beach-duckpool-thrift.jpg)

Wherever you roam, please keep dogs under close control around livestock and on the cliffs, and — on the farm — help us out by closing gates behind you.

## Practical tips for a Cornwall holiday with your dog

A few things we've learned over the years:

- **Check tide times** before a beach trip — some beaches all but disappear at high tide. A quick search for the day's tides saves disappointment.
- **Pack for Cornish weather** — a towel (or three), a drying coat and paw-wipes make life easier after a muddy valley walk or a sandy beach.
- **Watch the cliffs** — the coast path is stunning but unfenced in places, so keep dogs close near the edge.
- **Mind the livestock** — you may meet our cows and sheep on the farm walks; they're harmless but best given a wide, calm berth on a lead.
- **Bring their own bed & bowls** if it helps them settle — a familiar smell makes a new place feel like home fast.
