There is something special about December in North Cornwall. The summer crowds are long gone, the coast path is wild and empty, and Bude settles into a proper festive mood with twinkling lights, carol singing and independent shops full of gifts. It is the perfect time to gather the people you love for a get-together before the big day, and Woodlands Manor Farm is made for exactly that.

A pre-Christmas break lets you enjoy all the magic of the season, the markets, the walks and the cosy evenings, without the rush of Christmas Day itself. Whether it is grandparents, grown-up children, old friends or the whole extended family, here is why Woodlands is the perfect place to celebrate together, and what to do while you are here.

## A festive base for family and friends

Our [holiday cottages](/bude-holiday-cottages/) come in a real variety of sizes, from snug boltholes for two up to the [Manor House](/the-manor-house/), which sleeps twelve. There is plenty to keep every generation happy on site, too. Our [heated indoor pool, games room and soft play](/on-the-farm/) are a godsend on a wintry afternoon, and the children (and plenty of grown-ups) love a wander round to [meet and feed the animals](/on-the-farm/), from the sheep and alpacas to the pigs and ducks. Woodland walks start right from the gate, and the beach is only a couple of miles down the lane.

## Festive things to do in and around Bude

The run-up to Christmas in Bude is full of character. Dates shift a little each year, so do check [Visit Bude's Christmas in Bude guide](https://www.visitbude.info/christmas-in-bude/) for all the latest festive events before you travel, but these are the celebrations to look out for.

### Christmas lights and late-night shopping

Bude switches on its Christmas lights in late November, and the town centre is lovely to wander of an evening. The independent shops, galleries and delis are perfect for unhurried gift shopping, and many open late on selected evenings in December. Come early for the best finds and support some brilliant local makers while you are at it.

### An Enchanted Christmas at The Castle

The Castle Heritage Centre, right on Bude's seafront, usually hosts a festive weekend in early December with light displays, live music, craft workshops, artisan stalls and a visit from Father Christmas. It is a warm, family-friendly day out and a good one to have up your sleeve if the weather turns.

![A festive Christmas market in Bude, North Cornwall](/images/blog/pre-christmas-celebrations-bude/christmas-market-bude.jpg)

### Christmas markets

As well as the market at The Castle, keep an eye out for the Crackington Haven Christmas market, usually held mid-December at the village institute. It is a quieter, friendlier affair full of handmade crafts and festive stalls, and it pairs beautifully with a blustery coastal walk.

### Carols and live music

Throughout December you will find carol evenings and live music in Bude's pubs, hotels and public spaces, including performances from the Bude Metric Brass Band. Check the local boards and the [Christmas in Bude listings](https://www.visitbude.info/christmas-in-bude/), then round off the evening with supper somewhere cosy. Our guide to the [best places to eat in Bude](/places-to-eat-in-bude/) has our own favourites.

### A festive light trail at RHS Rosemoor

A short drive over the border into North Devon, RHS Garden Rosemoor runs its magical Glow light trail through the gardens in the run-up to Christmas. Wrap up warm, book ahead, and enjoy the illuminations and soundscapes. It is a real treat for all ages.

![An illuminated winter light trail in the gardens near Bude](/images/blog/pre-christmas-celebrations-bude/festive-light-trail.webp)

### Bracing coastal and woodland walks

December is one of the best times to have the North Cornwall coast to yourself. Blow away the cobwebs on the South West Coast Path towards Northcott Mouth, Sandymouth or Widemouth Bay, or keep it gentle with a stroll through the woodland around the farm. There is no better way to earn your mince pie. You will find our favourite routes in our guide to [beaches and walks near Bude](/beaches-and-walks-near-bude/).

![Walking the South West Coast Path near Bude on a crisp winter day](/images/blog/pre-christmas-celebrations-bude/coast-path-winter.webp)

### For the brave: the Christmas Day Swim

If you are staying on over Christmas itself, the Christmas Day Swim at Crooklets Beach is a much-loved local tradition, where hardy souls dash into the Atlantic to raise money for the Bude Surf Life Saving Club. Even if you would rather keep your feet dry, it is a wonderful, heart-warming thing to cheer on from the shore with a flask of something hot.
