There is something rather magical about Bude at Christmas. The crowds of summer have gone, the Atlantic rolls in under big winter skies, and the little town wraps itself up in fairy lights, carols and the smell of mulled wine. Add a bracing beach walk, a cosy pub and a sea that a few brave souls will actually swim in on Christmas morning, and you have a proper Cornish festive break.

So what's on in Bude this Christmas? Here is our round-up of the best festive events in and around Bude for 2026, from the Christmas Fayre and carol nights to the famous Christmas Day Swim at Crooklets.

## Bude Christmas Fayre: Saturday 5 December

The centrepiece of the Bude calendar, the **Bude Christmas Fayre** takes place on **Saturday 5 December 2026, 11:00am to 4:00pm**, spread across **The Parkhouse Centre** and **The Castle**. Expect a proper community day out: local makers and stalls, live choirs, family workshops and Winter Queen storytelling to keep the children spellbound.

It is the perfect way to pick up a few handmade gifts while soaking up the festive atmosphere. You can find the full programme at [The Castle Bude](https://www.thecastlebude.co.uk/christmas-fayre-2026/).

## Carols, live music and local gatherings

The villages around Woodlands get into the spirit early, so there is plenty on before the big day.

- **Stratton:** pre-Christmas celebrations at St Andrew's Church in Stratton on **28 November** kick the season off.
- **Morwenstow and Kilkhampton:** local festive gatherings at [The Bush Inn at Morwenstow](/bush-inn-morwenstow-best-pub-cornwall/) and the Kilkhampton Grenville Rooms on **5 December**.
- **Christmas at Ebbingford:** a lovely outdoor evening on **Friday 18 December 2026, 6:30pm to 7:30pm**, with Bude Metric Brass, carols, a torchlit treasure hunt and refreshments, all in aid of local charities.
- **Live music at the Preston Gate Inn:** seasonal entertainment and live bands throughout December, including Bude Metric Brass, Ross Kelly and The Wilder Days Country Band.

For the latest listings as the dates firm up, [Visit Bude](https://www.visitbude.info/christmas-in-bude/) keeps a running guide to what's on.

## Down at Crooklets: the Christmas Day Swim and more

![The wide sands of a Bude beach in winter light](/images/blog/pre-christmas-celebrations-bude/coast-path-winter.webp)

[Crooklets Beach](/bude-blue-flag-beaches-2026/) becomes the heart of festive Bude over Christmas week.

**The Christmas Day Swim** is the big one: a long-running community tradition on **Friday 25 December 2026**, with the warm-up from around **10:45am** and the dash into the chilly Atlantic at about **11:00am**, traditionally without wetsuits. It draws a big, cheerful crowd and raises money for the **Bude Surf Life Saving Club**. You do not have to swim to join in: wrap up warm, bring a flask, and cheer the brave from the shore.

There is usually more going on at Crooklets too:

- **Crooklets beach hut Christmas market:** a festive market at the beach huts, typically over an early-to-mid December weekend, with local makers, mulled wine, cider, mince pies and music. Do check local Bude listings for the confirmed 2026 dates.
- **Festive sauna sessions:** Ocean Soul Sauna runs **daily from 21 December 2026 to 3 January 2027** at Crooklets, perfect for a warm-up after a cold-water dip or a blustery beach walk.

If the sea feels a step too far, there is an easier swim close to home: our own [heated indoor pool](/about-woodlands-manor-farm-holiday-cottages-with-a-pool/) is kept at a toasty 30°C and open every day of the year, Christmas Day included.

## A festive day out further afield: Clovelly

About forty minutes up the coast in North Devon, the car-free fishing village of **Clovelly** puts on one of the best festive events in the area.

The **RNLI Christmas Light Switch On** falls on **Sunday 6 December 2026, from 2:00pm**, with carols down the famous cobbled street, a brass band procession, Father Christmas, a carol service and lights switch-on at 5:30pm, and fireworks over the harbour at 6:15pm. Entry is around £10 per person, free for under-16s, in aid of Clovelly RNLI. There is a **Boxing Day barbecue** on **Saturday 26 December** too, and the steep, traffic-free street down to the working harbour is a magical festive stroll at any time over the holidays. See [clovelly.co.uk](https://www.clovelly.co.uk/) for tickets and times.

Saturday 5 December 2026, 11:00am to 4:00pm, across the Parkhouse Centre and The Castle, with stalls, live choirs, family workshops and Winter Queen storytelling.
